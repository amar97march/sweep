The first public release of Sweep: a free, open-source app that cleans, protects and explains your Mac.

**Watch the 1:45 demo:** https://youtu.be/7XYMyBQJFTM

### What's inside

- **Checkup**: one scan for junk, security, maintenance and app updates.
- **Junk Files**: caches, logs, Xcode and developer junk, local AI models and Docker images, all reviewed before anything is removed.
- **Disk Map**: a live bubble map of any disk or folder, What Grew since your last scan, and System Data explained.
- **Apps**: complete uninstalls, leftovers of trashed apps, and a check for Intel-only apps before Rosetta goes away.
- **Updates**: every app in one list, from developers, the App Store and Homebrew, with Update All.
- **Health**: a live score, Battery Care, SSD health, a security checklist and a drive speed test.
- **Activity**: camera and microphone history, background items, battery drain and data use per app, app usage time, and apps that can see what you type.
- **Menu bar**: live readings, alerts and Keep Awake.

### Install

1. Download **Sweep-0.1.0.dmg** below, open it, and drag Sweep to Applications.
2. This build isn't notarized yet. On first launch, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to Sweep.
3. Give Sweep **Full Disk Access** for complete scans.

Requires macOS 14 or later.
